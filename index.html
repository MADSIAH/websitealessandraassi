Biography

Alessandra Assi, pianist and piano teacher trained through lifelong study under teachers of the Russian pianistic school, has performed both as a soloist and in ensembles in numerous concert halls across Italy and Switzerland. Her appearances include venues and events such as the Sporting Club of Monza, Piano City Milano, and the LAC in Lugano.

She has received numerous awards in national and international competitions, including the Andrea Baldi Competition in Bologna and the Val Tidone International Music Competition.
